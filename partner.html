From first message to first upload.

The order every partnership follows. Timelines inside each step are agreed with your team.

  1. InquiryTell us your platform, preferred model and the genres you need.
  2. SamplesWe send opening chapters and outlines in your preferred format.
  3. TermsWe agree the licence or buyout terms for the titles you pick.
  4. DeliveryEdited manuscripts are delivered on the schedule we agreed.
Can we read chapters before committing?

Yes. Sampling chapters and story outlines are part of every conversation, in whatever format your editorial team prefers to review.

Do you offer both long-form and short-form?

Yes. We distribute long-form serialized novels and short-form titles, so you can scale the format that fits your reader base and release schedule.

Which genres can you supply?

High-performing romance tropes such as Contract Marriage, Mafia, Sports Romance, Rebirth and Dragon Fantasy, plus male lead categories: Mountain Descent, Apocalypse, Urban Son-in-Law and Demon. Christmas and holiday titles are coming soon.

Who holds the rights to your titles?

Our writers are paid for their work and assign rights to dramreads in writing, so every title we offer is ours to license or sell.

Can we request a custom catalog?

Yes. Tell us the tropes, word counts and volume you need in the inquiry form and our editorial team will put a selection together.
