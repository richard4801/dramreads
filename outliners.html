Do I write the chapters?

No. You write outlines only. Our writers turn approved outlines into finished chapters.

Which genres can I outline?

Any of our core genres: Contract Marriage, Fake Dating, Revenge, Mafia, Sports Romance, Rebirth and the other trending romance tropes, plus male lead categories such as Mountain Descent and Urban Son-in-Law.

How much will I earn?

$0.50 to $5.00 per approved chapter outline. Your rate is set by the quality of your outlines, so it can rise as your work does. A month's batch is 50 to 200 approved outlines.

When do I get paid?

Bimonthly, for every approved batch. An outline approved in February is paid between March 25th and 30th.

What if a batch isn't approved?

Payment is for approved batches only, so our editors tell you what to fix before the next one.
